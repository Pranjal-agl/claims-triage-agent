## Rate actions
Illinois auto premiums will rise by 6% for renewals from 1 July 2015. Ohio and Indiana rates are unchanged. Do not communicate this before the regulatory filing is accepted.

## Umbrella cover
From Q3 2015 we stop writing new umbrella limits above 6,000,000 USD.

## Fraud target
The board target is a confirmed fraud rate below 20% of claims by the end of 2015.
