## Personal data
Policyholder names, email addresses, zip codes, street addresses of incidents, sex, education, occupation, hobbies, relationship and capital gains or losses are personal data. Policy numbers, claim IDs, ages and states are not personal data on their own.

## Who may see personal data
Claims analysts work with de-identified data only. Regional adjusters may see personal data for policies in their own region only. The Special Investigations Unit and the Head of Claims may see all personal data.

## Fraud determinations
SIU fraud determinations (fraud_reported) are visible to SIU, the Head of Claims and analysts working on aggregate statistics. Regional adjusters must not see fraud determinations, so that settlement decisions are not biased.
