## Cycle-time targets
Target time from first notice of loss to settlement: Ohio 30 days, Illinois 35 days, Indiana 30 days. Total losses have a 45-day target in every region.

## Escalation contacts
Each region has one claims manager. Complaints unresolved after 10 business days go to the Head of Claims.
