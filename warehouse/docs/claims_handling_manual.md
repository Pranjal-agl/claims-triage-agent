## First notice of loss
Every new claim must be acknowledged to the claimant within 2 business days of first notice of loss (FNOL). An initial reserve must be set within 5 business days.

## Settlement authority
Regional adjusters may settle claims up to 25,000 USD. Claims from 25,001 to 75,000 USD need a claims manager's approval. Claims above 75,000 USD need approval from the Head of Claims.

## Theft claims
Vehicle theft claims require a police report. A theft claim without a police report must not be settled and is referred to the Special Investigations Unit (SIU).

## Total losses
Claims with incident_severity 'Total Loss' are handled by the total-loss desk, which arranges salvage and pays the vehicle's actual cash value.

## Claimant contact
Adjusters contact claimants only about claims in their own region, using the contact details on the policyholder record.
