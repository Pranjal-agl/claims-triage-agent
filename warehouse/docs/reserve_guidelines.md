## Initial reserves
Set the initial reserve at 110% of the first estimate for Major Damage claims and at 100% for Minor and Trivial Damage. For Total Loss claims, reserve the vehicle_claim amount plus any injury_claim.

## Reserve reviews
Reserves must be reviewed every 30 days while a claim is open, and immediately if bodily injuries are reported after FNOL.
